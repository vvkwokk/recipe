Letter Back to Tomorrow

Author: Vianna Kwok

Prep Time: 1 minute

Cook Time: 30 minutes

Total Time: 31 minutes

Yield: 1 Letter

Description

In this recipe, you will write a letter to your future self to look back on. When writing the letter, consider this: What do you want to accomplish? What are you current values and goals? Who do you enjoy spending time with? Where do you see yourself in 1 year? 5? 10?

Ingredients

  • 1 piece of clean paper
  • silence or music
  • 1 writing utensil
  • 1 ponderful head

Instructions

  1. Clear your workspace of all screens and distractions. Set the vibe by playing thought-inducing music or silence.
  2. Close your eyes and bring yourself into focus. Notice your colors, emotions, memories and how it makes you feel right now.
  3. Begin writing to your future self. Tell your future self where you are, what you hope for, what you fear, and what you want you never to forget. It is important that you write without judgment and are not too self-conscious.
  4. Once the time is up (or your page is full), fold the paper in half. Write today’s date and time on the outside alongside a date in the future when it should be opened. Put it away somewhere (i.e. tucked in a notebook).
  5. Best enjoyed years from now : )
